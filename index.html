SITE-SPECIFIC SOLUTIONS

Septic systems, built with the site in mind.

A septic system isn't just a design on paper. It has to work with the property, the regulations and the realities of construction.

EO works with the project designer to bring a construction perspective into the conversation. That means considering access, excavation, grading, equipment, existing landscape and the finished property while respecting the approved design and applicable requirements.

  • Construction input early in the process
  • Site-specific access and excavation planning
  • Practical efficiencies where permitted
  • Minimizing unnecessary disturbance
  • Restoration with the finished property in mind

Sometimes the best solution isn't simply the most conventional one.
