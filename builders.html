The Builder's Problem

Too many handoffs. Too much figuring it out.

Civil work has a way of becoming fragmented. One contractor clears the property. Another handles excavation. Someone else deals with drainage. Then everyone has to coordinate around each other.

EO is built around a different approach: take responsibility for the civil scope and think through how the pieces need to work together.

That means better communication, better sequencing and a site that's ready for the next trade when we're finished.
