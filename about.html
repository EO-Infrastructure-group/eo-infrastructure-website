About EO Infrastructure Group

Construction should be done properly.

EO Infrastructure Group was built around a simple idea: do the right thing, think beyond the immediate scope and create the most value possible for the project.

Our Approach

More than getting the work done.

Civil construction is connected work. The decisions made during clearing, excavation, servicing, drainage and grading can affect everything that comes after it.


EO looks at the bigger picture. We work to understand what the project needs, how the work needs to be sequenced and what can be done better before the equipment even starts moving.


The goal isn't simply to complete our scope and leave. It's to leave the project in a better position for the next phase of construction.
