Our Capabilities

Civil work that connects the pieces.

From breaking ground to leaving the site ready for the next trade, EO brings the equipment, planning and construction approach required to get the work done properly.

01

Excavation & Earthworks

Site preparation, bulk excavation, building excavation, cuts and fills, earthmoving and subgrade preparation. We move, place and shape material with the next phase of construction in mind.

02

Site Servicing

Civil infrastructure required to connect a project to existing services, including utility trenches, service connections and supporting excavation.

03

Sewer & Water

Underground civil infrastructure including sanitary sewer, storm sewer and water services, coordinated with the broader site servicing requirements.

04

Drainage & Culverts

Culverts, drainage infrastructure and practical water management solutions designed around existing site conditions and the finished project.

05

Septic Systems

Septic installation and supporting site work for properties where private sewage systems are required, coordinated with the overall site plan.

06

Demolition & Clearing

Remove what's there so construction can begin. Land clearing, site preparation and demolition of existing structures and site features.

07

Backfill & Grading

Structural backfill, material placement, rough grading and final site shaping to prepare the property for foundations, paving, landscaping and the next trade.

08

Restoration

Complete the site properly. Restoration, cleanup, grading and preparation so the project can transition cleanly into its next phase.
